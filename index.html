Ask on WhatsApp
How much does a website cost?
Most projects fall between PKR 15,000 and PKR 80,000. A simple business website starts at PKR 15,000, while e-commerce stores and custom systems sit higher. Share your requirements and you will get a clear quote before any work starts.
Do you provide domain and hosting?
Yes. Every package includes a domain and hosting for the first year, fully set up and connected to your website.
How long does a website take?
It depends on the scope. Smaller business websites and landing pages are the quickest, while e-commerce stores, web apps and mobile apps take longer. You get a clear timeline together with your quote.
Can you build e-commerce websites?
Yes. Stores include product pages, categories, cart and checkout, order notifications and an easy way for you to add or update products.
Do you build mobile apps?
Yes. Shehroz 4411 builds mobile apps for Android and iOS, designed to match your brand and your website.
Do you provide WhatsApp integration?
Yes. Websites can include a WhatsApp chat button, pre-filled order or enquiry messages and product-specific links, so customers reach you in one tap.
What happens after the first month of support?
Your website keeps running as normal. Reasonable changes are free during the first month after launch. After that, each additional website change is billed at $50 per change.
